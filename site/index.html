How it works

From a buyer's spreadsheet to a reviewed, export-ready answer set.

1 Upload your policies

Add PDFs, Word docs, or text. We split them into searchable, citable passages.

2 Upload the questionnaire

Drop in the buyer's XLSX. Questions are detected automatically.

3 Agents draft and verify

A drafter answers only from your evidence. A critic rejects anything unsupported.

4 Review and export

Approve or edit, then export answers with citations back into the original file.

Built so a wrong answer never ships

Security answers are commitments. The product is designed around that.

Every answer is cited

Each draft points to the exact passage it came from, or says "Insufficient evidence".

Critic and escalation

Weak support, missing citations, and sensitive topics (breaches, certifications, legal) go to a human.

Human approval

Nothing is final until a reviewer approves it. Approvals are logged.

Answer library

Approved answers are reused for repeat questions, so each questionnaire gets faster.

Privacy by design

Emails, phone numbers and IPs are masked before any language-model call.

Gap and freshness insights

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers.

Will it make things up?

It is built not to. Drafts must cite retrieved passages, and a critic blocks uncited or unsupported claims. When evidence is missing, the answer says so and a human decides.

Which formats do you support?

Policies: PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown. Questionnaires: XLSX today. Portal and Word/PDF export are on the roadmap.

Where does my data go?

The live demo runs entirely in your browser. The full product sends text to a language model only after masking personal data, and is designed for zero-retention provider settings. See the Security page for what exists today and what is planned.

Are you SOC 2 certified?

No. We are an early-stage company and will publish our certification status honestly as it changes.
